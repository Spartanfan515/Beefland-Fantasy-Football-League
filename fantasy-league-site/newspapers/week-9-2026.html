Austin Gauss's Puka Bites Back led the league at 161.56 and beat Zay it with Flowers by 31.10 to improve to 7-2 and move into second place behind Njigba's in Paris. Kenneth Walker III did the heaviest lifting with 38.3 points, the best score by any starter in the league, on 143 total yards and three touchdowns on 24 touches. Puka Nacua added 37.0 on 14 catches (19 targets) for 150 yards and a touchdown, and Tee Higgins chipped in 20.2. Those three account for 95.5 of the 161.56.

Zack Rollis's team got 37.5 from CeeDee Lamb (10 catches, 195 yards and a touchdown) and still finished 31 points short. The one cloud over the win is Nacua, who was hurt on the Rams' final offensive snap of their 20-13 win at Washington and was scheduled for a knee MRI on Monday (see Injury Recap). The league average was 115.06, and the Week 9 scoring was low across the board: only five teams topped 120.

Most Valuable Players

Kenneth Walker III · RB · KC
143 Total Yds · 3 TD · 24 Touches

Started for Austin Gauss's Puka Bites Back and led all starters in the league with 38.3 points, his best score of the season. His three touchdowns are the biggest reason Puka Bites Back topped 160 points for the first time since Week 4.

CeeDee Lamb · WR · DAL
10 Rec · 195 Yds · 1 TD

Started for Zack Rollis's Zay it with Flowers and posted 37.5 points on 10 catches on 16 targets with 195 yards and a touchdown, the second-best score by a starter in the league. It still wasn't enough for a Zay it with Flowers team that fell to 6-3.

Puka Nacua · WR · LAR
14 Rec · 150 Yds · 1 TD

Started for Austin Gauss's Puka Bites Back and scored 37.0 points on 14 catches (19 targets) for 150 yards and a touchdown, adding 20 rushing yards on two carries in Los Angeles' 20-13 win at Washington. Gauss will be holding his breath on the knee MRI.

Ja'Marr Chase · WR · CIN
35.5 Pts

Matthew Golden · WR · GB

Jordan Love · QB · GB (Bench)
372 Pass Yds · 3 Pass TD · 1 Rush TD

His 18.26-point starter, Drake Maye, gave Love Her Big Judkins a 4.50-point loss to Joshin Around, a game Love would have flipped by about 14 points. Love Her left 74.8 on its bench, the most in the league, and C.J. Stroud's 42.36 on Ben Schon's bench was the highest score of anyone in the league this week.

Injury Recap

KNEE · MRI Monday

Puka Nacua

WR · LAR · Started by Puka Bites Back (Austin Gauss)

Nacua was hurt on the Rams' final offensive snap of their 20-13 win at Washington after a monster game (37.0 points). The Rams didn't believe he tore his ACL, but he was set for an MRI on Monday, and coach Sean McVay reportedly indicated he is likely headed to injured reserve with a PCL/MCL sprain and turf toe.

HAND · Left In First Quarter

Josh Jacobs

RB · GB · Started by Geezers n' Beaters (Jon Hurlburt)

Jacobs left Green Bay's game in the first quarter with a hand injury after four carries and 0.5 points. His status was unclear heading into Week 10 against Minnesota. Geezers n' Beaters scored just 67.78 and lost to Njigba's in Paris by 48.06.

CONCUSSION · Ruled Out

Jaylen Waddle

WR · DEN · Rostered by Costa Rico (Jacob Ayriss)

Waddle left before halftime with a concussion and was ruled out. He has a bye in Week 10 and will have extra time to heal. Costa Rico was unaffected, scoring 139.44 to beat Uncle Lamb's.

HAMSTRING · Inactive

George Pickens

WR · DAL · Started by Gibbs Me Shelter (Evan Lamb)

Pickens was listed questionable with a hamstring injury, had restricted practices for a second straight week, and was inactive for Dallas's game at Indianapolis. Lamb started him anyway and was assessed a chug (see Most Wanted).

BACK · Pregame Spasms

Ladd McConkey

WR · LAC · Rostered by White Men Can't Jump (Ben Schon)

McConkey played no snaps after back spasms in pregame warmups. He was on Ben Schon's bench, so the damage was limited, and White Men Can't Jump still won 130.78 to 110.58.
