Zach Crook's What ACL? was the only team in Beefland to clear 140 points, and Drake London did it without even finding the end zone — 9 catches on 10 targets for a career-high 194 yards as Atlanta stunned Green Bay on the road, 35-14. George Kittle (26.2), Christian McCaffrey (21.6) and Deebo Samuel Sr. (20.4) all padded the total further, and What ACL? didn't need to sweat it: the 142.84 demolished Blake Beachnau's Kareem in my Hunt 142.84-69.74, the widest margin of the week by a mile. Beachnau's lineup ran straight into a buzzsaw of real-world injury news — Justin Jefferson and De'Von Achane both left their games early (full details below in Injury Recap).

The highest score of any team that still lost this week belonged to Jacob Ayriss's Costa Rico, whose 115.34 — led by Bijan Robinson's 37.3 — wasn't quite enough to catch Austin Gauss's Puka Bites Back in the tightest game of the week, a 14.32-point final. Full breakdown below in Miracles Come True.

Miracles Come True

Jahmyr Gibbs · RB · DET
164 Total Yds · 3 TD

Started for Evan Lamb's Gibbs Me Shelter and turned in the single best individual performance in Beefland this week — 99 yards and two touchdowns on the ground, plus 65 yards and a third score through the air, as Detroit beat the Jets 31-24. It wasn't enough to save the matchup, though: Jon Hurlburt's Geezers n' Beaters still won by 21.02 to move to a perfect 3-0 on the season.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba · WR · SEA
128 Rec Yds · 2 TD

Adam Kahler's Njigba's in Paris got another monster week from its namesake — 10 catches for 128 yards and two touchdowns in a 33-31 shootout loss to Washington, Smith-Njigba's second straight 35-plus-point fantasy week after his 49.5-point explosion in Week 2. Njigba's in Paris is 3-0 to start the season, cruising to a 22.22-point win over Uncle Lamb's this week.

Juwan Johnson · TE · NO
8 Rec, 53 Yds · 2 TD

Started for Austin Gauss's Puka Bites Back and turned in the best tight end week in the league — 8 catches on 8 targets for 53 yards and two touchdowns in New Orleans' loss to Las Vegas. Johnson and fellow Saints tight end Noah Fant each found the end zone twice in the same game, only the third time in modern NFL history two tight end teammates have both done that. Paired with a 28.66-point night from Sam Darnold, it was exactly enough to survive the tightest game of the week, a 14.32-point escape over Costa Rico.

Injury Recap

IR · Season-Ending

De'Von Achane

RB · MIA · Started by Kareem in my Hunt (Blake Beachnau)

Achane's leg gave out on a non-contact cutback less than five minutes into Miami's loss to Kansas City, tearing his ACL. He walked off under his own power, finishing at 1.7 points, but the Dolphins have already ruled him out for the rest of the 2026 season.

OUT · 3+ Weeks

Baker Mayfield

QB · TB · Started by Gibbs Me Shelter (Evan Lamb)

Mayfield dislocated his throwing-hand thumb in Tampa Bay's loss to Minnesota and didn't return, finishing with 12.28 points already banked. Head coach Todd Bowles confirmed he's out a minimum of three weeks, with rookie Jalon Daniels stepping in under center.

QUESTIONABLE · Week-to-Week

Breece Hall

RB · NYJ · Started by Joshin Around (Adam Schon)

Hall planted awkwardly on a tackle in the fourth quarter of the Jets' 31-24 loss to Detroit and grabbed his thigh on the way to the sideline. He finished with just 32 yards on 13 carries, and the team is calling it a quad injury and labeling him week-to-week — Braelon Allen is next up if he can't go.

QUESTIONABLE · Awaiting Update

Terrance Ferguson

TE · LAR · Started by Costa Rico (Jacob Ayriss)

Ferguson's ankle sent him to the sideline twice against Denver — he tried to play through it, came back, then was pulled again in the second quarter and taken for X-rays. He finished with a season-low 1.9 points, and the Rams haven't yet said whether he'll be limited heading into Week 4.

QUESTIONABLE · Week 4

Justin Jefferson

WR · MIN · Started by Kareem in my Hunt (Blake Beachnau)

Jefferson lasted just six offensive snaps before an ankle sprain sent him to the locker room in Minnesota's 23-16 win over Tampa Bay, finishing with two catches for 32 yards and 5.2 points. Kevin O'Connell called it a straightforward sprain with "nothing long-term" — swelling will decide his Week 4 workload.

QUESTIONABLE · Week 4

Travis Etienne Jr.

RB · NO · Started by Njigba's in Paris (Adam Kahler)

Etienne pulled up grabbing his hamstring on a 16-yard run midway through the third quarter against Las Vegas — an aggravation of an issue that had limited him in practice all week. He was ruled questionable to return and never went back in, finishing with 9 points.

QUESTIONABLE · Week 4

Jalen Coker

WR · CAR · Started by White Men Can't Jump (Ben Schon)

Coker left Carolina's game at Cleveland early with a quad injury after catching 2 of 4 targets for 18 yards. NFL Network's Ian Rapoport called it a minor strain, and the Panthers are optimistic he'll be good to go for Week 4 against Detroit.

CLEARED · No Time Missed

Pat Freiermuth

TE · PIT · Started by Gibbs Me Shelter (Evan Lamb)

Freiermuth was briefly checked for a left arm/shoulder injury against Cincinnati but missed only one series, returning to finish with 2 catches for 30 yards. More of a scare than a setback — no long-term concern heading into Week 4.
