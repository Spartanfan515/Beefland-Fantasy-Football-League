Zack Rollis's Zay it with Flowers took the league high for the second straight week, and this time the engine was a Pittsburgh back who did it all. Jaylen Warren piled up 101 rushing yards and 87 receiving yards, plus a rushing touchdown and a receiving touchdown, in the Steelers' 34-22 win over Indianapolis, and his 48.80 points were the biggest single-player score anywhere in Beefland. Isaiah Likely added 32.4 from the tight end slot with a career-high 11 catches and two touchdowns in the Giants' 27-27 overtime tie with Washington, and Aaron Rodgers chipped in 25.0 through the air.

Then came Monday night. James Cook III carried 25 times for 111 yards and two touchdowns and added a 7-yard catch in Buffalo's 37-34 overtime win over the Rams, good for 24.8 points. Those four alone account for 131.0 of Rollis's 163.10 — and the margin over Evan Lamb's Gibbs Me Shelter was only 5.60, so Cook's night by itself was worth more than four times the gap. Lamb's 157.50 was the highest score by a team that lost and would have beaten every other team in the league this week, powered by 39.7 from Jahmyr Gibbs (151 rushing yards in Detroit's 45-21 win over Arizona), 30.0 from George Pickens, and 25.0 from Brock Bowers.

Worth noting: Rollis and Adam Schon swapped stars before the week, with Rollis sending Kyren Williams (his Week 4 MVP) and Matthew Golden to Joshin Around for CeeDee Lamb (Schon's 43.3-point star) and Breece Hall. Williams and Golden combined for 41.9 points for Schon; Lamb gave Rollis 2.9 and a bruised quad (see Injury Recap), and Hall sat on the bench at 0.0. Rollis still won the week.

Miracles Come True

Puka Nacua · WR · LAR
17 Rec, 138 Yds · 1 TD

Started for Austin Gauss's Puka Bites Back (yes, that one) and turned Monday night into a career night: a career-high 17 catches, capped by a 2-yard over-the-shoulder touchdown from Matthew Stafford that put the Rams up 20-19 early in the fourth quarter of their 37-34 overtime loss to Buffalo. His 39.7 points were more than a quarter of Puka Bites Back's 139.66. Add Jayden Daniels (34.26, with 100-plus rushing yards in the Commanders' 27-27 tie with the Giants) and Malik Nabers (31.1 from that same game) and that trio produced 105.06 points, enough to survive Ben Schon's White Men Can't Jump by 6.34 even with Christian Watson's 41.3 (143 yards and two touchdowns in Green Bay's 31-7 win over Chicago) on the other side.

James Cook III · RB · BUF
25 Car, 111 Yds · 2 TD

Started for Zack Rollis's Zay it with Flowers and delivered 24.8 on the same Monday night stage, with a rushing performance that included two touchdowns and a 7-yard catch. Cook did fumble in overtime to hand the Rams a chance, but Harrison Mevis's 48-yard attempt was blocked by Greg Rousseau and Josh Allen drove Buffalo to Tyler Bass's 45-yard game-winner. Rollis needed every point of it to hold off Gibbs Me Shelter by 5.60.

Isaiah Likely · TE · NYG
11 Rec · 2 TD

Also on Rollis's roster, Likely set a career high with 11 catches and found the end zone twice in the Giants' 27-27 overtime tie with Washington, turning a tight end slot into 32.4 points — the second-biggest score on the league's best team.

Drake Maye · QB · NE
26/34, 374 Yds · 4 Pass TD, 1 Rush TD

Started for Jack Callahan's Love Her Big Judkins and posted 40.46 points as New England rolled Las Vegas 45-17 — the third-highest starter score in the league and good for well over a third of Callahan's 105.96 in a 21.02-point win that snapped his four-game losing streak.

Injury Recap

Samuel went down with a hamstring injury in the third quarter of San Francisco's 32-29 win over Seattle and was ruled out for the rest of the game, finishing with 2.5 points. It was the second starter Crook lost on the day (see Roman Wilson).

OUT · Week 6

Roman Wilson

WR · PIT · Started by What ACL? (Zach Crook)

Wilson left Pittsburgh's win over Indianapolis late in the second quarter with a hand/finger injury (early reports also mentioned his wrist) and was ruled out for the rest of the game. He has already been ruled out for Week 6 against Tampa Bay. He scored 0.0 (see Most Wanted).

CONCUSSION PROTOCOL

T.J. Hockenson

TE · MIN · Started by Geezers n' Beaters (Jon Hurlburt)

Hockenson was hurt on Minnesota's opening possession after a 7-yard catch, went to the locker room for a head-injury check, and never returned in the Vikings' 23-13 win over New Orleans. He is in the NFL's concussion protocol and finished with 1.7 points.

WEEK-TO-WEEK

David Montgomery

RB · HOU · Started by Puka Bites Back (Austin Gauss)

Montgomery injured his groin during Houston's 26-3 win and did not return, finishing with 1.1 points. He is considered week-to-week, and Woody Marks took over the starting duties.

WEEK-TO-WEEK

Tre' Harris

WR · LAC · Started by Costa Rico (Jacob Ayriss)

Harris exited early with a hamstring injury in the Chargers' Week 5 loss to Denver and is considered week-to-week. He finished with 3.6 points in Costa Rico's 36.42-point loss to Njigba's in Paris.

OUT · Multiple Weeks

TreVeyon Henderson

RB · NE · Started by Kareem in my Hunt (Blake Beachnau)

Henderson made an early exit from New England's 45-17 win over Las Vegas with an ankle injury that has since been diagnosed as a high-ankle sprain. He is expected to miss multiple weeks. He finished with 4.8 points.

QUAD BRUISE · Week 5

CeeDee Lamb

WR · DAL · Started by Zay it with Flowers (Zack Rollis)

Lamb suffered a quad bruise during Dallas's Thursday-night loss to Tampa Bay and was listed as questionable to return. He finished with 2.9 points for his new team, Zay it with Flowers, after arriving in the Breece Hall trade with Joshin Around.
