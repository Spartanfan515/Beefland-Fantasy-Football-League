Adam Kahler's Njigba's in Paris hung 167.78 on Ben Schon's White Men Can't Jump — the third-highest score of the 2026 season, behind only Zack Rollis's 179.76 in Week 4 and Jon Hurlburt's 175.88 in Week 2, and the biggest margin of the year at 90.66 points. Jaxon Smith-Njigba led the way, catching nine of his 12 targets for 150 yards and a touchdown in Seattle's 20-3 win over Kansas City for 33.3 points. Trey McBride added 29.1 on 10 catches for 111 yards and a touchdown, and Joe Burrow tossed four touchdowns for 28.88. Those three alone account for 91.28 of the 167.78.

The most impressive part is what Kahler didn't use. Bo Nix scored 34.18 on his bench, more than any starter on his own roster, and the 55.2 points sitting there were the third-most in the league. It didn't matter: the win lifted Njigba's in Paris to 6-1, in a three-way tie for first with Zay it with Flowers and Puka Bites Back, who both won as well. The league average was 119.18, and at the other end Ben Schon's 77.12 was the second-lowest total of the week.
