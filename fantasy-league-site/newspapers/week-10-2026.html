Zach Crook's What ACL? scored 195.72, the highest total of the 2026 season, passing the 193.68 Adam Kahler posted in Week 8. And Kahler was the victim: Crook beat Njigba's in Paris by 92.06 points, the largest margin of the year, to move to 6-4, while Njigba's in Paris fell to 8-2. Christian McCaffrey did the damage with 58.3 points, 12.1 more than any other player in the league this week. Dak Prescott added 35.42 (343 passing yards and five touchdowns for Dallas) and Deebo Samuel Sr. had 30.9, which included a 70-yard rushing touchdown. Those three account for 124.62 of the 195.72.

The depth did the rest. Drake London (18.9), George Kittle (13.4), the Lions D/ST (13.0) and Jadarian Price (11.4) were all solid, and What ACL? left another 43.8 on the bench, including Stefon Diggs's 20.3 and Ryan Flournoy's 19.1. The league average was 128.3, up from 115.06 in Week 9, and two teams topped 190 (Gibbs Me Shelter's 192.78 was the third-highest score of the season).

Injury Recap

KNEE · MCL/PCL Sprains, Turf Toe

Puka Nacua

WR · LAR · Rostered by Puka Bites Back (Austin Gauss)

Sean McVay said Nacua suffered MCL and PCL sprains and turf toe, and he is likely headed to injured reserve. He won't return before Week 15 at the earliest. Nacua was on Gauss's bench in Week 10 as Puka Bites Back lost to Kareem in my Hunt, 93.36 to 102.16, and fell to 7-3.

ANKLE · Suspected Fracture

Drake Maye

QB · NE · Started by Love Her Big Judkins (Jack Callahan)

Maye was ruled out midway through the second quarter after an ankle injury on a tackle by Aidan Hutchinson, with a suspected fracture. Reports said he is out indefinitely, if not for the season, leaving Jack Callahan (3-7) with a hole at quarterback; Tommy DeVito would be next up in New England.

GROIN · Sidelined

Kenneth Walker III

RB · KC · Rostered by Puka Bites Back (Austin Gauss)

Walker was listed questionable with a groin injury and was out of Kansas City's lineup, a week after a 38.3-point game. Emmett Johnson filled in and scored 37.1 points for Costa Rico, while Walker sat on Gauss's bench.

FINGER · Surgery

Josh Jacobs

RB · GB · Rostered by Geezers n' Beaters (Jon Hurlburt)

Jacobs fractured a finger and was set to have surgery this week; he is expected to miss a maximum of three weeks. He was on the bench for Geezers n' Beaters, who lost to Zay it with Flowers 167.00 to 107.72.

HAMSTRING · Left in Fourth Quarter

A.J. Brown

WR · NE · On IR for Costa Rico (Jacob Ayriss)

Brown had just 4.7 points before leaving New England's loss to Detroit in the fourth quarter with a hamstring injury, with a timeline update expected early in Week 11. He has appeared in only three games this season.
