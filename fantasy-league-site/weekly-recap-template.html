King Beef ran away with Week 1, and it wasn't close. Evan Lamb's 184.60 points led the league by double digits, powered by a monster night from his stud receiver, who turned a short slant into a 58-yard house call in the second quarter and added a second score on a goal-line fade in the fourth. He commanded a third of the team's targets and was never more than a play away from breaking the game open.

It's the kind of week that makes a 12-team league feel like a formality — one bell-cow performance, a full trophy case of stat-sheet stuffing, and a Monday morning group chat that had nothing left to argue about.
